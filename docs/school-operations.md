# School Operations & Module Integration

## Module Overview
School operations are divided into specialized command hubs, each focusing on a specific institutional domain.

### 1. Academic Operations
- **Management of Structure**: Academic Years, Terms, Classes, and Sections.
- **Curriculum**: Subject catalog and teacher assignments.
- **Student Lifecycle**: Enrollment, transfers, and year-end promotions.

### 2. Financial Management
- **Invoicing**: Automatic and manual fee generation based on student class/level.
- **Collections**: Tracking payments (Cash, Bank, Online) and recording allocations.
- **Reporting**: Outstanding balances and collection summaries.

### 3. Attendance & Safety
- **Daily Roll Call**: Subject-wise or daily attendance marking by teachers.
- **Guardian Alerts**: Automated High-Priority notifications (SMS/Email) on student absence.
- **History**: Full audit trail of student presence.

### 4. Examination & Results
- **Assessment Lifecycle**: From scheduling to score entry and validation.
- **Publication**: Moderated approval workflow before results are visible to parents.

### 5. Library & Transport
- **Logistics**: Management of physical assets (books, vehicles) and their assignments to students.
- **Safety**: Route planning and driver/member tracking.
