# Privacy & Data Protection

## Principles
- **Data Minimization**: Collect only what is necessary.
- **Purpose Limitation**: Use data only for educational and school management purposes.
- **Transparency**: Clear communication with users about data usage.

## User Rights
- **Right to Access**: Users can request a copy of their personal data via the Security Settings.
- **Right to Rectification**: Users can request corrections to their data.
- **Right to Deletion**: Subject to legal retention requirements for academic and financial records.

## Data Classification
See `security-architecture.md`.

## Retention Policies
Standard retention periods:
- **Audit Logs**: 365 days
- **Security Events**: 90 days
- **Sessions**: 30 days
- **Student Records**: Indefinite / Until 7 years post-graduation
- **Financial Records**: 7 years
